Our story

A place to find your people.

Founded in 2022 by a small group of students seeking more than a ranked queue, F4our Gaming has grown into a community hub for casual players, competitive teams, casters, designers and event organisers.

We organise welcoming game nights and ambitious inter-university events, with one belief at the centre: every player deserves a squad.

Vision

To bring together everyone who is interested in Honor of Kings through one welcoming website and community.

Mission

To provide clear and complete competition information in one place, so users do not need to search across different social media platforms.
